# Review Desk

**Before you change how any screen looks, read [docs/design.md](docs/design.md).** It is the
design system: what makes a screen a document or a workbench, what a key is, the words a
decision is read under, and what a page may and may not do to what it draws. The values it
is built from are in `web/app/assets/css/tokens.css`.

**For everything else, the [README](README.md) is the reference** — the commands, the data
contract, the record and its migrations, the layout of the workspace, the checks to run
locally, and the end-to-end test.

This file holds no rules of its own, only these two pointers, so that it cannot come to
disagree with what it points at.
