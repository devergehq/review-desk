How a review flows

Seven layers. Each writes one thing to the record. You act at two of them.
1 · automatic
Lint
Description budget, working notes, template shape. No model.
Writes: lint result
2 · agent, then you
Understand
The problem in plain words from the ticket and the code, with a diagram. No judgement yet.
Writes: brief · you confirm or correct it
3 · agent
Alternatives
The ways this problem could be solved, written before the diff is read.
Writes: option map
4 · agent
Solution map
What they built, laid over the options. What it gains and what it gives up.
Writes: approach verdict
5 · agent
Code review
The eight lenses, the repository's rules, and a blind second reader.
Writes: findings with evidence
6 · agent, then you
Triage and draft
Nits are batched into one ticket. The rest becomes a draft you preview and edit.
Writes: draft · you approve it to post
7 · automatic
Harvest
Author replies, later commits and repeat findings are read back from GitHub.
Writes: outcomes · proposes rules

Who talks to whom

Dispatcher
Sees the review request and starts a session. Unchanged.
Review session
Reads the rules, runs the layers, writes each result through the command line.
Review Desk
Owns the record. Shows you what is waiting. Harvests outcomes.
You
Confirm the brief. Edit and approve the draft. Decide on rules.
Your approval goes back to the review session, which posts to GitHub. Nothing else posts in your name.
